## Why Mystery Nights are a separate purchase (not bundled into Pro)

Speakeater is three products in one app:

1. **Cooking**. photograph your fridge, get recipes. Free tier + Pro upsell on volume.
2. **Cocktails**. 6,539 drinks including 2,848 pre-Prohibition manuscript transcriptions. Free tier + Pro upsell on volume.
3. **Mystery Nights**: multiplayer party games, completely separate product category.

The cooking and cocktail sides are subscription-economic: people use them weekly, they're a habit, Pro retention works there. Mystery Nights are events: you host one every few weeks, maybe every few months. That's transactional, not subscription. Charging per-game matches how people actually consume them.

Pro members get one Mystery Night free as a teaser. If the mystery side ends up out-performing the cocktail and cooking sides post-launch, we revisit the bundle. The founder rate at $30 a year locks in regardless of how the bundle evolves.

After the campaign ends, yearly Pro retails at $45. $30 is the founder rate, and it is grandfathered for the operational lifetime of the app. Every backer at the $30 tier or above locks $30 a year forever, as long as their subscription stays active. Renewals never roll to $45 for founders.

A subscription that does not get more expensive is the version of permanence that survives a solo developer's lifecycle better than a one-time lifetime SKU.
