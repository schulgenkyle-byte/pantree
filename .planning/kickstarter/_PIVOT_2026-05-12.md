# Kickstarter Pivot Note — 2026-05-12

**What changed:** The product positioning pivoted from "fifty curated cocktail menus with mystery nights as a bonus" to **"multiplayer mystery party games anchored in pre-Prohibition cocktail history."**

**Why:** Game category beats content category. WTP 3-5x higher. Comp set moves from Paprika/Yummly to Jackbox/Hunt-A-Killer. Group purchase + group play + group share. The cocktails and menus stay — they're the production design now, not the headline product.

**Stretch ladder (post-pivot):**

| Threshold | Unlock |
|---:|---|
| $25k | Speakeasy World Tour (10 international party menus) |
| $40k | Expanded Codex (2,846 cocktails as free PDF) |
| $50k | Mystery Nights Expansion (+25 interactive mysteries, total 30) |
| $60k | Voice mode (radio-narration for beats + recipes) |
| $80k | Custom Mystery Builder (player-authored mysteries) |
| $100k | **Video Clue Pack** — AI-generated period-styled video snippets delivered as twist clues. Pivotal info, hints, character monologues. Some players get videos others don't see. Adds a cinematic multimedia layer to the game. |

**Pricing model change:** Mystery Nights are a separate add-on, not bundled into Pro. Non-Pro members can buy individual mystery games and individual menus. Pro members get one free Mystery Night as a teaser. If Mystery Nights end up out-performing the rest of the product, we revisit the bundle.

**App backend now includes:** A Cloudflare Durable Object (`GameRoom`) handling per-game state, WebSocket connections, beat timeline scheduling, and per-character message routing. One DO instance per active game, keyed by a 4-letter room code. 100+ concurrent games is the design target. Cost: under $20/mo at 1,000 games/month.

**Speakeater app pillars (still three):**
1. Cooking — photograph your fridge, get recipes (existing, unchanged)
2. Cocktails — 6,539 drinks including 2,848 pre-Prohibition manuscript transcriptions (existing, unchanged)
3. **Parties — Mystery Nights + Curate-a-Party menus (NEW headline)**

The cocktail and food sides remain part of the app. The game side is the Kickstarter angle.
