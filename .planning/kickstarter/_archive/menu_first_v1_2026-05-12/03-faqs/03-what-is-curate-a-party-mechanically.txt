QUESTION:
What is Curate a Party, mechanically?

ANSWER:
A menu is five drinks, six small plates, a host timeline, a shopping list, and a music note. Each menu is dated, sourced, and named for the bar that inspired it. You download one to your phone. You shop. You host.

Every menu ships in two registers. Faithful uses the period ingredients exactly. Modern-flexible offers substitutions for vegetarian, gluten-free, or alcohol-free hosts. The mocktail set covers the alcohol-free swaps with real temperance-era recipes.
