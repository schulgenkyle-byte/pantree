## Pricing math (for press/reviewers, dry version)

Retail post-launch: $4.99 a month or $45 a year for Pro. Single menus $5 each for non-Pro.

The $30 tier locks the founder rate forever. $15 off retail every year for as long as the subscription stays active. Grandfathered for the operational lifetime of the app. Capped at 500 backers across the campaign.

The $49 tier prices each menu at $0.38 against a $5 retail. Sweet-spot tier. Same Founder Rate as the $30 tier.

The $99 tier earns its extra fifty dollars over the $49 in name-on-the-wall recognition and a direct support channel for the first twelve months. The Founder Rate is the same as the $30 tier. There is no lifetime Pro.

The $250 tier covers Lulu print-on-demand ($14 US printing, $5 shipping) with margin to spare. No inventory.

The $500 and $1,000 tiers are time-for-money. Hard caps keep solo-dev fulfillment sane.

## Why $30/yr is exclusive to backers

After the campaign ends, yearly Pro retails at $45. $30 is the founder rate, and it is grandfathered for the operational lifetime of the app. Every backer at the $30 tier or above locks $30 a year forever, as long as their subscription stays active. Renewals never roll to $45 for founders.

Capped at 500 backers across the campaign. After the cap is hit, the founder rate is closed.

There is no lifetime Pro tier. Speakeater does not sell one. The Founder Rate at $30 a year, forever, is the strongest commitment the campaign makes. A subscription that does not get more expensive is the version of permanence that survives a solo developer's lifecycle better than a one-time lifetime SKU.
