I am one person. Schedule risk is the obvious failure mode.

The mitigations are in place. The app exists. The hard work of building the platform is done. The Kickstarter funds the new feature plus runway. Worst case: the Curate-a-Party UI ships late, the menus arrive as emailed PDFs instead of in-app. Backers get the menus either way.

The Founder Rate at the $30 tier and above is grandfathered for the operational lifetime of the app, stated in the privacy policy. If Speakeater shuts down, all Pro features unlock for everyone via the final app update. There is no lifetime Pro tier. The Founder Rate at $30 a year, forever, is the strongest commitment the campaign makes.
