# Kickstarter Strategy — Pre-Launch Campaign

**Goal:** $15,000 / 30 days
**Launch target:** 14 days from this doc (to allow prelaunch buildout)
**Date written:** 2026-05-12

---

## Why Kickstarter, why now

Pre-launch Android app. No live users yet, so the conversion-first growth gate (5% save-to-cook minimum) is moot. There is no funnel to gate against.

Kickstarter solves three pre-launch problems at once:

1. **Validate willingness-to-pay before Play Store launch.** If 200 strangers won't pay $4 for a single Curate-a-Party menu, $4.99/mo retention won't work either. Better to find out now.
2. **Build the launch-day email list.** Standard pre-launch is 500-2k emails. Kickstarter backers convert at higher rates than cold-email signups because they have already opened a wallet.
3. **Fund the launch costs.** Solo dev burn (Anthropic API, Cloudflare, Play Console fees, icon design, video shoot) is real. $15k covers 6 months of runway.

The launch this Kickstarter enables IS the Play Store launch. The campaign is the warm-up act.

## Target backer (priority order)

1. **Cocktail history nerds.** Own a copy of *Imbibe!* by David Wondrich, follow @greatestbarintheworld on Instagram, host Negroni Sept parties. They will pay for curated era-specific menus.
2. **Home entertainers in their 30s.** Host dinner parties 4-6 times a year. Pinterest-save Prohibition aesthetic content. Will pay for the "I'm a host with a curated speakeasy app" social proof.
3. **Cooking-app churners.** Tried Yummly, Paprika, SuperCook. Found them clinical. Prohibition aesthetic is the wedge that gets them to try one more.

NOT targeting: general cooking-app audience. They would rather use Yummly free.

## Success thresholds

| Outcome | $ amount | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| Floor | $15k | Campaign funds. Validation achieved. Launch list built. |
| Target | $25k | First stretch unlocks: Speakeasy World Tour (10 international menus). |
| Stretch | $40k | Manuscript Codex unlocks (50 transcribed pre-Prohibition cocktail manuscripts as digital book). |
| Reach | $60k | Voice mode unlocks (recipes read aloud in 1920s radio announcer voice). |
| Failure | Below $10k by day 15 | Pull campaign. Better to cancel publicly than to fail publicly. |

## What this campaign is NOT

- Not a presale of an unfinished app. The app exists. 5,036 cocktails, 23,743 food recipes, 1,361 mocktails, 6,539 drinks total, 2,848 pre-Prohibition cocktails, 2,846 with original-source text preserved — already in the production D1 database. Pro billing now works end-to-end as of 2026-05-12.
- Not seeking lead investors or strategic partners. Consumer pre-launch.
- Not selling 50 menus that don't exist yet. The campaign promises 50 by Play Store launch + 10 stretch via World Tour. Solo-dev write-rate target: 10 menus/month for 5 months.

## Sequencing (30-day campaign)

| Day | Phase | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| -14 to -1 | Prelaunch | Email capture live on speakeater.com. Target 500 emails. |
| 0 | Launch | Blast email list. Personal network outreach. |
| 1-7 | Momentum | 30% of goal by day 7. First stretch tease. |
| 8-22 | Maintenance | Mid-campaign update (day 15). Influencer push. |
| 23-30 | Close | Final 48-hour urgency. Stretch goal push. |

## Risks

- **Solo-dev fulfillment.** Physical menu booklet at $250 tier introduces print logistics. Capped at 50 backers, fulfilled via Lulu print-on-demand. No inventory risk.
- **Aesthetic over-promise.** Prohibition era requires authentic-feeling visual design. Two options for hero video: hire designer for a one-day shoot, or use Speakeater Studio Remotion templates I already built.
- **App not on Play Store yet.** Backers worry "will this actually ship?" Mitigation: weekly campaign updates with build screenshots. Play Console Internal Testing invite for $25+ backers.

## Post-campaign metrics to track

- Conversion of backers to Play Store downloads (target: 90%)
- Conversion of $0-tier email signups to backers (target: 8%)
- Curate-a-Party menu purchase rate post-launch (target: 30% of Pro users buy at least one menu in first 90 days)
- Net Promoter Score from backer survey at day 60 post-launch (target: NPS > 30)
