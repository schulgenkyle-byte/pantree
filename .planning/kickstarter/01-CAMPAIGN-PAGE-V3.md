# A murder mystery in your kitchen. Eight phones. One night you'll talk about for a year.

## It is 1924. The theatre critic at the Algonquin is dead.

Eight friends at your table. Eight phones. The cocktails are mixed. Each phone lights up with a character, a role, and one secret only that player knows.

You tap Begin. For three hours, Speakeater runs the night across all eight phones at once. Twists arrive privately. Clues cascade publicly. Each player drives part of the story. The reveal lands at the last cocktail.

You don't host the mystery. The app does. You pour the drinks.

---

## Five Mystery Nights at launch

**Murder at the Algonquin.** Manhattan, 1924. The Round Table set. The theatre critic is dead.

**The Bootlegger's Wife.** Chicago, 1929. A basement bar after a raid. Someone tipped the feds.

**Last Toast at the Ritz.** Paris, 1925. Frank Meier's bar at closing. The wealthy patron just collapsed.

**Heir to the Pendennis.** Louisville, 1923. Derby Eve. The old colonel's will reading is tomorrow.

**The Vanishing Socialite.** West Egg, 1926. A young woman left the ballroom at midnight.

Each one ships with five characters, a host playbook, the twists, the reveal, and the period-correct cocktail and food menu underneath the game.

## Why I built this

I'm Kyle. Solo developer. Huntsville, Alabama.

I photographed every American bartender's manuscript from 1862 to 1923 and hand-transcribed 2,846 cocktails from those photographs. I built the native Android app and the multiplayer backend. The five mysteries are written.

This Kickstarter funds the multiplayer engine and six months of runway so it all ships on Google Play June 10, 2026.
